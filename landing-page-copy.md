## Why switch (EN)

**The price of QuickBooks is more than a month of groceries.**
QuickBooks starts at $456/year. For a food truck doing $40k a year, the bookkeeping software costs more than the food. CuentaLibre is $0 — forever, MIT open source.

**Spanish isn't a translation. It's the product.**
Every screen, every hint, every tax explanation — written in Spanish and English from the first tap. Switch languages mid-setup, even mid-sentence. QuickBooks has a Spanish mode; nobody would call it Spanish-first.

**Zero jargon.**
QuickBooks makes you feel like you need a bookkeeper to use the bookkeeping software. We say "count the money when it arrives," not "cash-basis accounting." Help text on every screen, in plain words.

**Nonprofits get the 990 helper.**
A walkthrough that gets a tiny nonprofit ready for its 990. QuickBooks doesn't do this at any price. Neither does anyone else in this lane.

**Your books stay yours.**
Local-first, open source: no cloud lock-in, no surprise price hikes, no "verify your account to continue." Save your books to a file; open a backup whenever you need.

## What we don't do yet (EN — say it out loud, it becomes trust)

- **No automatic bank feeds.** Your bank doesn't talk to the app; you enter or import what matters.
- **No payroll.** Track contractor payments and 1099s — we don't run payroll itself.
- **Desktop only for now.** Windows, Mac, Linux. No phone app yet.
- **Accountants live in QuickBooks.** We hand them a clean CSV export instead of native integration.

## Lo que aún no hacemos (ES — decirlo en voz alta genera confianza)

- **Sin conexión automática al banco.** Tu banco no habla con la app; tú registras o importas lo importante.
- **Sin nómina.** Registra pagos a contratistas y 1099s — no procesamos la nómina.
- **Solo de escritorio por ahora.** Windows, Mac, Linux. Aún no hay app para teléfono.
- **Los contadores viven en QuickBooks.** Les entregamos un CSV limpio en lugar de integración nativa.

## README top section (paste above the current README's feature bullets)

### EN — Why CuentaLibre

**Free, open-source, bilingual accounting for small business owners — built for people who run a business, not accountants.**

QuickBooks starts at $456/year and assumes you speak accounting and English. CuentaLibre is $0, Spanish-first (every screen, every hint, toggleable anytime — even mid-setup), and explains everything in plain words. Local-first: your data stays on your computer in SQLite, no account, no cloud, no subscription. Includes a 990 walkthrough for nonprofits — something QuickBooks doesn't do at any price.

Honest caveats: no automatic bank feeds, no payroll, desktop-only (Windows/Mac/Linux) for now, and accountants get a clean CSV export instead of native integration.

### ES — Por qué CuentaLibre

**Contabilidad gratuita, de código abierto y bilingüe para dueños de pequeños negocios — creada para personas que tienen un negocio, no para contadores.**

QuickBooks cuesta desde $456 al año y asume que hablas contabilidad e inglés. CuentaLibre cuesta $0, el español va primero (cada pantalla, cada ayuda, cambiable cuando quieras — hasta en medio de la configuración) y todo se explica en palabras sencillas. Primero local: tus datos se quedan en tu computadora en SQLite, sin cuenta, sin nube, sin suscripción. Incluye un asistente 990 para organizaciones sin fines de lucro — algo que QuickBooks no hace a ningún precio.

Con honestidad: sin conexión automática al banco, sin nómina, solo de escritorio (Windows/Mac/Linux) por ahora, y los contadores reciben un CSV limpio en lugar de integración nativa.
